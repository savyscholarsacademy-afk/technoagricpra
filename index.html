About TECHNOAGRICPRA (PVT) LTD

Bridging Hands-On Field Expertise with Modern Agricultural Technology.

Headquartered in Marondera, Mashonaland East Province, TECHNOAGRICPRA (PVT) LTD (trading as TECHNOAGRI PRECISION) is a Zimbabwean agricultural services and technology company dedicated to improving farm productivity, resilience, and market readiness.

We believe technology only succeeds in rural agriculture when paired with trusted human presence on the ground. Our field officers work directly alongside farmers—delivering practical agronomy, livestock management, and soil testing while equipping farms with simple digital tools to track inputs, manage records, and connect with formal buyers.

Our Mission

To equip Zimbabwean farmers with accessible extension expertise, quality inputs, and transparent digital farm records that unlock sustainable yields and formal economic participation.

Our Approach

Combining local field officers, community Village Hubs, and cargo-tricycle logistics with offline-capable mobile and SMS advisory tools.

  • Field demonstrations & farmer training workshops

Livestock Production & Animal Health Management

  • Vaccination schedules, dipping & biosecurity plans

  • Linkages to vetted veterinary inputs & livestock buyers

Climate-Smart Crop & Horticulture Consulting
